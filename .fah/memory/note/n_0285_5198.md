# Note: n_0285_5198

Owner directive (2026-10-12): pre-merge validation is ALWAYS a single queue (validation mutex = 1 by design). Duplicating CI runs is forbidden — do NOT strip ai_validated/ai_validating labels or fire manual ticks to force re-arms just to speed a PR through; the BEHIND→silent-update→revalidate churn after every main merge is the accepted cost of the design, not a bug to work around. gh-837 validationConcurrency stays at 1 unless the owner raises it. Earlier same-day context: owner also allows canceling hung runs >3-4h and applying `blocked` to deprioritize PRs when a factory-critical PR needs the lane.
